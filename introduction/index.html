Introduction

Shinhyeong Choe is a Postdoctoral Scientist in the Department of Environmental Chemistry at Eawag, the Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science and Technology. He is a Hyundai CMK Science & Technology Fellow and a 2026 ACS CAS Future Leader.

Research interests

  • How polymers biodegrade and move through natural and engineered environments
  • Interactions between polymers and microbiomes, and data-driven prediction of material persistence
  • Sustainable, bio-based, and environmentally safe polymer materials
