My research asks how biodegradable plastics and water-soluble polymers break down in the environment, and what controls the rate.

- **Fate of polymers in the environment.** How biodegradable plastics and water-soluble polymers break down, and where the carbon ends up.

- **Structure and property effects.** How a polymer's chemical structure and physical properties govern its biodegradation and mineralization.

- **Rates, not verdicts.** Measuring biodegradation rates and separating the steps — polymer breakdown, depolymerization, and full mineralization — rather than treating degradation as a single pass/fail outcome.

- **Environmental context.** How environmental conditions and prior exposure history change degradation across different settings.

- **Predictive models.** Building data-driven models that link polymer properties to environmental biodegradability.

- **Design and testing.** Applying mechanistic understanding of biodegradation to sustainable polymer design and to biodegradability test methods that hold up.

- **Who is doing the work.** Combining metagenomics and metatranscriptomics with degradation measurements to identify the microorganisms and pathways actually responsible.
