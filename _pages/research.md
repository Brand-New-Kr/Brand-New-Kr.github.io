My research interests are:

- Understanding the environmental biodegradation and fate of biodegradable plastics and water-soluble polymers
- Investigating how polymer structure and physicochemical properties influence biodegradation and mineralization
- Quantifying biodegradation kinetics and distinguishing key processes from polymer transformation and depolymerization to ultimate mineralization
- Evaluating how environmental conditions and exposure history affect polymer biodegradation across different receiving environments
- Developing data-driven and predictive approaches to connect polymer properties with environmental biodegradability
- Translating mechanistic understanding of biodegradation into sustainable polymer design and scientifically robust biodegradability assessment
- Integrating metagenomic and metatranscriptomic analyses with biodegradation measurements to identify active microbial populations and pathways driving polymer degradation
