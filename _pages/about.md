I study how polymers biodegrade in the environment — biodegradable plastics and water-soluble polymers in particular — and I am interested in making that a quantitative question rather than a pass/fail one. The work combines environmental chemistry, polymer science, and metagenomics.

I am a postdoctoral scientist in the Department of Environmental Chemistry at Eawag, the Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science and Technology, in Dübendorf. I am a Hyundai CMK Science &amp; Technology Fellow and a 2026 ACS CAS Future Leader.
